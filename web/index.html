How it works

  1. DropAll your camera videos and the audio in one box: phone, camera, 360.
  2. Name your camerasTell Montara who each camera shows. For 360 cameras, click the points of view you want.
  3. Pick the framesMontara cuts on the beat and shows you every shot. Swap the ones you don't like.
  4. RenderWith the Montara watermark, your own logo, or nothing at all.
